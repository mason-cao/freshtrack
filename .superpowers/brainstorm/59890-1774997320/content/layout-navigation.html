Key Behaviors

Bottom Tabs (Mobile)
Fixed at bottom with Framer Motion layoutId sliding active indicator. 4 tabs: Home, Pantry, Recipes, Stats.

72px collapsed rail with icon + label. Switches from bottom to side at md breakpoint. Logo at top.
FAB (Quick Add)
Floating sage-green button above bottom tabs. Framer Motion spring scale on press. Opens the add item dialog.
Page Transitions
Framer Motion AnimatePresence with slide + fade between tab pages.
